2. Hosting and server log data

This website is a static site served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you open a page, GitHub processes technically necessary data: your IP address, the date and time of access, the address requested, and information about your browser and operating system.

Purpose: to deliver the site and protect the service against abuse and attacks. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; my legitimate interest is operating the website securely and reliably.

Retention: I do not receive or store these log data myself. According to GitHub, it keeps them only as long as needed for security, to meet legal obligations and to resolve disputes; GitHub does not state a fixed period. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Transfer to the USA: GitHub processes the data in the USA. GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework; the transfer relies on the European Commission's adequacy decision (Art. 45 GDPR).

3. Cookies, analytics and third-party content

This website sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device, uses no analytics, tracking or marketing services, and loads no fonts, scripts or other content from third parties.

4. Contact by email

If you write to me by email, I process your email address, your name and the content of your message in order to answer your enquiry.

Legal basis: If your enquiry concerns a contract or steps prior to one, for example using MausMatrix in your research group, the legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR. For all other enquiries it is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR; my legitimate interest is answering enquiries addressed to me.

Retention: I delete your enquiry once it has been dealt with and no follow-up questions are to be expected, as a rule no later than twelve months after the last message. If the enquiry leads to a contract, I keep the business-relevant correspondence for as long as commercial and tax law requires (up to six or ten years).

Service provider: Email is processed by Google Workspace (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) under a data processing agreement. A transfer to the USA is possible; Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR).

5. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20). An informal email to the address above is enough.

You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of North Rhine-Westphalia (LDI NRW), Kavalleriestraße 2-4, 40213 Düsseldorf, Germany, www.ldi.nrw.de.

6. Your right to object (Art. 21 GDPR)

Where I process your data on the basis of legitimate interests (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR), you have the right to object to this processing at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation. I will then no longer process the data unless I can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, rights and freedoms, or the processing serves the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims. No particular form is required; the simplest way is an email to konradschweizer@mausmatrix.com.

7. Obligation to provide data, no automated decisions

You are not obliged to provide any data. Without the technically necessary connection data, however, the site cannot be delivered, and without contact details I cannot answer an enquiry. No automated decision-making or profiling takes place.

8. The MausMatrix software

This policy covers the website. The MausMatrix software itself runs in your browser; papers and project data are stored on your device. If you connect an AI model, the texts you ask about are sent to the provider you chose, under your own account and its terms. I receive none of this data.
